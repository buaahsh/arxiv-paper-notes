# Paper Note — Attention Is All You Need

- **Title:** Attention Is All You Need
- **arXiv ID:** 1706.03762
- **Summary:** This paper proposes the Transformer, a novel sequence transduction model based entirely on attention mechanisms, dispensing with recurrence and convolutions, and achieves state-of-the-art results on machine translation benchmarks while being significantly more parallelizable and requiring less time to train.
